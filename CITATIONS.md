## Element Biosciences tools

- [bases2fastq](https://docs.elembio.io/docs/bases2fastq/) — base-call demultiplexing and FASTQ generation for the AVITI / AVITI24 systems.
- [cells2stats](https://docs.elembio.io/docs/cells2stats/) — AVITI24 cytoprofiling: cell segmentation, target assignment and per-cell statistics.
- [elembio-diss-tools](https://github.com/Elembio/elembio-diss-tools) — 3' untargeted RNA read filtering, feature assignment and counting.
- [elembio-spatialdata-build](https://github.com/Elembio/elembio-spatialdata-build) — assembles the per-run SpatialData (`.zarr`) object.
